# Contributing

Thanks for taking part. Whether this is your first pull request or your five hundredth, you're welcome here.

## Sharing an entry, step by step

1. **Fork** this repo. Click **Fork** at the top right of the page. You now have your own copy.
2. **Make your folder:** `submissions/YYYY-MM/your-github-username/`. For November 2026 that's `submissions/2026-11/your-github-username/`.
3. **Add your work.** Charts, notebooks, scripts, a link to a dashboard: whatever you made.
4. **Add a `README.md`** to your folder, copied from [SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md](SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md). Include at least one image so your entry can go in the gallery.
5. **Open a pull request** from your fork. Put your level in the title, e.g. `[Beginner] How Leeds handled the 2008 crash`.

Not used Git before? You can do all of this in the browser. In your fork, go to `submissions`, click **Add file** then **Upload files**, and type your folder path into the file name box.

## The rules

- **Only change your own folder.** If your pull request changes other files, you'll be asked to split it.
- **Don't upload the raw data.** Link to the source instead, and keep your folder under 10MB.
- **Credit the data source** as shown in each brief.
- **Real analysis only.** Your numbers should come from the data. Using AI tools to help is fine. Just say so in your README.
- **Write for everyone.** Explain what you found in plain language, as if to a friend who doesn't work with data.
- **Be kind.** Feedback in reviews and Discussions should help people improve.
- **Late entries are welcome.** They just won't make that month's gallery.

## Gallery picks

At the end of each month a few entries are featured in the main README. Picks are about clear thinking and a good finding, not complexity. A simple chart with a sharp point can beat a complicated model.

## Suggesting an idea

Got a question about house prices and the economy, or a dataset that would help answer one? Open an issue with the **Idea suggestion** template, or post in Discussions.

## Questions

Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/ShazMoghaddam/uk-house-prices-economy/discussions). Someone has probably had the same question.
